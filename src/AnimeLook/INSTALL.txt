Anime Look @VERSION@
for Kerbal Space Program 1.12.x

Every craft and kerbal squashed flat toward the camera, as the 3D models of drawn animation
are: from where you look nothing seems flat, but the perspective inside each model is gone
and it reads as a drawing that turns as you go round it. With ink lines and flat paint over
the picture.

INSTALL
  This mod needs Keystone 0.11 or later, the base it stands on, which is a separate download:
    https://spacedock.info/mod/4665/Keystone
  Copy Keystone's folder (Keystone) into your KSP's GameData folder, and the AnimeLook
  folder from this download beside it, and start the game. Without Keystone the game does
  not load this mod. One copy of Keystone serves every mod that uses it. To remove this
  mod, delete the AnimeLook folder.

USE
  It is on as installed: the squash in flight; the ink and the paint in flight, in the
  cockpit, in the buildings where craft are made and at the space centre. Its settings are
  in the Keystone window: the keystone button on the game's toolbar, or Alt-K. Then turn to
  Anime Look: the squash (how much depth is kept), the ink lines and the flat paint each
  have a switch.

  Alt-J holds the squash where the camera stands. Move the camera after it and you see
  every craft for the flat cut-out it is. Alt-J again lets go.

GOOD TO KNOW
  The craft are not changed: only how the flight camera and the crew's portraits draw them.
  Physics, the map and the cockpit view are as they were.
  The game finds the part under the pointer by its true shape: close to a craft a small part
  can be drawn a little off from where it is clicked.
  The game's flames and smoke are moved with the craft they come from. Waterfall's plumes
  are squashed with it. Beside Firefly, a craft keeps its depth while Firefly's glow of
  re-entry shows on it.
  In the buildings where craft are made there is the ink and the paint, on the craft and
  the building, and no squash.

WHERE IT WORKS
  Made on Windows 11 on Direct3D 11 and seen there, on the game alone and beside Scatterer,
  EVE, TUFX, Parallax Continued, Deferred, Waterfall, Firefly and KSP Community Fixes. On OpenGL (a Mac, Linux) the squash is the same code
  and has not been run; the ink and paint are not in this version for OpenGL.
